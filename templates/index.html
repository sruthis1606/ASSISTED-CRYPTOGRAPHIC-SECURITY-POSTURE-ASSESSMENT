Practical remediation

Common improvements for email-system configurations

  • Require modern transport encryption

    Disable TLS 1.0/1.1 and prefer TLS 1.3 or TLS 1.2 with AEAD cipher suites.

  • Replace weak cryptography

    Retire DES, 3DES, RC4, MD5, and SHA-1; review key sizes against current policy.

  • Maintain trusted certificates

    Use certificates with a valid trust chain and hostname; renew them before expiry.

  • Strengthen authentication

    Use modern authenticated submission, unique credentials, and multi-factor authentication.
